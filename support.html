Reminders & Notifications

How do I create a reminder?

Open Remember and select the category where you want to create an entry. Enter the details, choose a date or reminder time where available, and save.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

On your iPhone, open:

Settings > Notifications > Remember

Enable Allow Notifications and review your notification settings.

Also verify that your reminder has the correct date and time.

Premium Subscriptions

Remember offers optional Premium features through in-app subscriptions.

Subscription pricing and billing terms are displayed in the app before you purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name.
  3. Select Subscriptions.
  4. Select Remember, if listed.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Subscriptions purchased through Apple are managed through your Apple Account.

Having trouble with Premium?

If a purchase is not recognized, use Restore Purchases in the app if that option is available.

For additional assistance, email support@justisend.com .

Account & Sign-In Help

Forgot your password?

On the Remember sign-in screen, select Forgot Password and follow the instructions to reset it.

Cannot sign in?

Check your email address and password, verify your internet connection, and try again.

If the problem continues, contact customer support.

Account Deletion & Data Requests

If you wish to delete your Remember account and associated personal data, please contact our support team.

Email: support@justisend.com

Include "Remember Account Deletion Request" in the subject line and identify the account email address.

Our support team will explain the available deletion process and any applicable data retention requirements.

Do not send your password or sensitive personal documents by email.
