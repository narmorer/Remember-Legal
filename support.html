Reminders & Notifications

Create an entry in the appropriate category and save it. Where available, set a date and reminder time.

To receive alerts on iPhone, open Settings > Notifications > Remember and enable Allow Notifications.

Subscriptions

Remember offers optional premium features. Subscription pricing and terms are shown inside the app before purchase.

To manage or cancel an Apple subscription, open iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions, then select Remember.

If a previous purchase is not recognized, use Restore Purchases in the app, if available.
