Privacy Policy

Remember – Life & Reminders

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Remember – Life & Reminders ("Remember", "we", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile app.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Remember, information may include:

  • Email addresses used for account authentication.
  • User identifiers used to manage accounts and subscriptions.
  • Subscription and purchase information used to provide premium access.
  • Information you enter into the app, such as reminders, appointments, birthdays, grocery items, and saved places.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality.
  • Authenticate user accounts.
  • Manage subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • Save and display reminders and other information.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

4. Storage of Reminders

Reminder entries and other saved items are stored locally on your device in the current version of Remember. These entries are not currently synchronized to a cloud database by the app.

5. Third-Party Services

Remember uses the following service providers:

  • Supabase: Account authentication.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management and purchase entitlement verification.
  • Apple: App distribution and in-app subscription payments.

These providers may process information needed to deliver their services according to their own privacy policies.

6. Advertising and Tracking

Remember does not display third-party advertisements. We do not use your information for cross-app advertising tracking.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable measures and established service providers to help protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

You can remove saved entries through the app. Because reminder entries are stored locally, deleting the app may also remove locally stored information.

To request deletion of account-related information, contact us using the email address below. We will process requests in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

Remember is not specifically directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
